About Institution Portal

AYUSH SkillBridge provides institutions with a centralized platform to monitor student skill development, academician engagement, industry opportunities and placement activities. The portal supports data-driven decision making and helps institutions strengthen Academia-Industry collaboration for AYUSH-related education, skills and career development.
